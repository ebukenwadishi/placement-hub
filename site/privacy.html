Privacy

Placement Hub is a prototype. This page explains what happens to your information when you visit this website or try the demo.

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version

  • You don’t need to give us any personal information to read the website.
  • No cookies, no advertising, and no tracking or visitor statistics.
  • The demo uses made-up records, and what you do in it stays on your own device.
  • The demo uses made-up records. If you create an account, your name, email address and password are handled by Google Firebase so you can sign in.

Who we are

This website and the demo are run by Placement Hub, a prototype for supported accommodation in England. It isn’t a live service yet.

If you have a question about privacy, email .

We don’t have a contact address yet. One will be added to this page.

This website

Reading the website doesn’t need any personal information. We don’t use cookies, analytics or advertising tools.

Two outside services help show you the pages:

  • Netlify hosts the website. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and basic browser details to send you the pages, and keeps short-term logs to keep the service working and secure.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google’s servers, so Google receives your IP address.

The demo

  • You sign in with a shared demo account. No account is made for you, and you don’t give us your email address.
  • Everything you add or change is saved in your web browser on that device. It isn’t sent to us or anyone else.
  • To remove it, open About in the demo and choose Reset sample data, or clear this website’s data in your browser settings.
  • To create an account, you give your name, email address and a password. These are stored by Google Firebase Authentication, which we use to sign you in, confirm your email address and reset your password. Google may store this information outside the UK.
  • We use your email address only for account emails, like the confirmation link and password resets.
  • Everything else you add or change in the demo is saved in your web browser on that device. It isn’t sent to us or anyone else.
  • To remove your records, open About in the demo and choose Reset sample data, or clear this website’s data in your browser settings. To have your account deleted, contact us using the details under “Who we are”.

Please don’t enter real information about young people, staff or anyone else. The demo isn’t built to protect it.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you. We delete them once we no longer need them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. In most cases we hold nothing about you.

If you’re unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Before real use

Placement Hub isn’t ready for real information about young people or staff. Before it is, it needs a full privacy notice, a data protection review, and the other safeguards listed under “Needed before real use”.

Changes to this page

If we change this page, we’ll update the date at the top.
